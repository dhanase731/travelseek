Zafari is your ultimate Indian travel companion — built for explorers who want to go beyond the tourist trail.

🗺️ DISCOVER HIDDEN GEMS

Browse thousands of extraordinary places across India — from secret waterfalls in Northeast India to forgotten forts in Rajasthan. Every place is handpicked or contributed by real travellers.

🤖 YATRA AI — SMART TRIP PLANNER

Tell our AI where you want to go and your budget. Get a personalised day-by-day itinerary in seconds — places, routes, hotels and local food included.

🚨 SABDHAN SOS — TRAVEL SAFE

One tap sends your live GPS location to the nearest police station and hospital via SMS. Your emergency contact is alerted instantly. Works even in remote areas.

🚂 TRAIN TRACKER

Track your journey progress live on a real-time map. See exactly how far you are from your destination using Google Maps road distance.

🏘️ COMMUNITY CHAT

Join state and district travel rooms. Share tips, photos and experiences with fellow travellers from across India. Real-time WhatsApp-style chat.

🌟 ZAFARI PREMIUM

Unlock unlimited travel radius (up to 1000km), ad-free experience, and full AI trip planning. Plans from just ₹39/month.

📍 KEY FEATURES

• Nearby places finder with photos and details

• AI-powered personalised trip planner (Gemini)

• SOS emergency alert with live GPS

• State and district community chat rooms

• Train journey live tracker

• Contribute and share hidden gems

• Works across all 28 states and 8 UTs of India

Built in India 🇮🇳 for every Indian traveller.
